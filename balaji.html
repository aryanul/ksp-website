Overview

Contemporary living on Purbachal Road

Balaji offers well-planned 2 & 3 BHK apartments with four homes on each floor, arranged around a central lobby and lift. Every home has its own balcony and a generous drawing-dining space.

The project is a short walk from autos and buses, about a kilometre from The Gateway Hotel and the upcoming metro station, and minutes from Ruby General Hospital, DPS School and Acropolis Mall. Balaji is bank approved for home loans.

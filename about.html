Who we are

A Kolkata developer committed to quality and trust

K S Promoters Pvt. Ltd. is a real estate development company headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, dedicated to developing quality residential projects that reflect thoughtful planning, superior craftsmanship, and enduring value.

Founded in 2011, the company has steadily built its reputation on a foundation of integrity, transparency, and customer satisfaction. Every development is envisioned with a focus on modern lifestyles, efficient design, and sustainable quality, ensuring spaces that meet the aspirations of today's homeowners and businesses.

At K S Promoters, we believe that every project is an opportunity to create more than just buildings — it is an opportunity to build communities, foster relationships, and deliver experiences that stand the test of time. Driven by ethical business practices and a commitment to excellence, we continue to pursue meaningful growth while creating developments that enhance the urban landscape of West Bengal.

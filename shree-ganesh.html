Overview

Every home faces south

Shree Ganesh is a G+3 residential building with six 3 BHK apartments — two on each floor — above a stilt car park. Each home measures 1297 sq.ft. super built-up and includes a living room, separate dining, two toilets and a balcony.

Located just 200 metres off the Prince Anwar Shah Connector and the EM Bypass, the project was completed and handed over in April 2015. It was marketed by Envision.
